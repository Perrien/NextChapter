 A LITTLE CONTEXT

About these numbers

Current value is your share count multiplied by each investment’s latest published price, or an explicitly labeled intraday estimate.

Changes and growth history use adjusted prices, assume today’s holdings throughout, and assume payouts are reinvested. The history is scaled to today’s value; it does not reconstruct your actual past account balances.

Mutual funds publish a daily price. Between closes, a related ETF supplies an estimate. Active funds can differ from their proxy; individual holding details explain the match.

The long-range outlook repeats the past year’s growth at a steady monthly rate, with withdrawals at the start of each month. It excludes inflation, taxes, new deposits, and uneven market returns.
